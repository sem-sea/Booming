Key takeaways

Rarely: press releases made up 1.1% of AI citations in Muck Rack's May 2026 analysis of more than 25 million links, against 84% for earned media. A release helps when it leads to coverage, not as a source on its own.

Muck Rack analyzed more than 25 million links cited by ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini across 17 industries. Earned media, including journalism, academic, government and third-party sources, made up 84% of citations; journalism alone about 27% (Muck Rack, May 2026).

Press releases moved around. Their share rose from about 1.2% in July 2025 to 6% in December 2025, then fell back to 1.1% by May 2026 (Muck Rack). Wire-service releases alone went from 0.2% to 1% of citations in the second half of 2025 (GlobeNewswire release on Muck Rack data).

So a release is a weak source and a strong trigger. What matters is the coverage it creates.

What is the role of a press release in AI search?

A press release in AI search is mainly a trigger: it rarely gets cited itself, but the coverage it generates in trade media often does.

Muck Rack found releases appear about 3.5 times more often in answers about industry trends than in best-of answers. They help most when your news is the trend.

Which sources do AI assistants cite instead?

The preferred source type is earned media: journalism, academic and government pages, encyclopedias, social platforms and other companies' content, together 84% of citations.

How should B2B PR teams adapt?

Adapting PR for AI means measuring coverage in the outlets assistants cite, pitching trade media with data, and treating releases as one tool among several.

  1. Find the outlets assistants cite for your category by running buyer prompts.
  2. Pitch those outlets with data: a benchmark, a survey or a customer result.
  3. Keep releases factual and dated, so they hold up if they are cited.
  4. Put the full story on your own site, in HTML, with the numbers.
  5. Track AI mentions after each announcement, not only press clippings.

Frequently asked questions

Should we stop sending press releases?

No. Use them for real news and as a basis for pitches. Do not expect AI citations from the release itself.

Do wire services help AI visibility?

Wire releases were about 1% of citations at the end of 2025. Coverage that follows is worth far more.

Which outlets matter for B2B technology?

Muck Rack found trade publications outweigh general news in technology answers. Check which titles assistants cite for your prompts.

How fast do AI assistants pick up news?

Muck Rack saw journalism citations peak in the first month after publication.

Is paid placement worth it?

Paid and advertorial content made up 0.3% of citations. Earned coverage is the better investment.
