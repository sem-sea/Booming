Key takeaways

Claude cites less often but more deeply than other assistants: in Muck Rack's May 2026 data, it cited sources in 55% of responses and averaged about 13 citations when it did. Allow Claude-SearchBot and publish primary, well-sourced pages.

Claude behaves differently from ChatGPT. Muck Rack's analysis of more than 25 million links found ChatGPT cites in 96% of responses with about five citations each, Gemini in 82% with about eight, and Claude in 55% with about 13 when it cites (Muck Rack).

Its sources lean academic. PubMed Central was Claude's most cited domain, and U.S. News and World Report its top journalism outlet. Claude did not cite YouTube at all in the sample (Muck Rack).

Traffic is still small. In Leadfeeder's 2026 study of B2B websites, Claude sent 5% of AI referral visits, against 81% for ChatGPT (Leadfeeder).

What is Claude search?

Claude search is the web search capability in Anthropic's Claude that retrieves current pages to answer a question and lists them as sources.

Anthropic runs separate bots. Claude-SearchBot indexes pages for search answers, Claude-User fetches pages when a person asks, and ClaudeBot collects training data. Blocking Claude-SearchBot may reduce your visibility in Claude's search results (Anthropic).

How is Claude different from ChatGPT for B2B brands?

Claude's difference for B2B brands is depth over frequency: fewer answers include sources, but those that do cite many, often primary and academic ones.

How do you get cited by Claude?

Getting cited by Claude means being a primary source: original data, clear definitions, documented methods and pages that Claude-SearchBot is allowed to read.

  1. Allow Claude-SearchBot in robots.txt and your firewall.
  2. Publish primary material: your own data, methods and technical documentation.
  3. Cite your sources on the page; well-referenced pages fit Claude's research-style answers.
  4. Write precise definitions under question-shaped headings.
  5. Track Claude separately in your prompt tool, because its sources differ.
