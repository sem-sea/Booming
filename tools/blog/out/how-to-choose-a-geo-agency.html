What is a GEO agency?

A GEO agency is a marketing partner that improves how often and how accurately AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini cite your brand.

GEO stands for generative engine optimization. The term comes from a 2024 study by researchers at Princeton and partners, presented at KDD, which found that content changes can lift a source's visibility in generative engine answers by up to 40% (Aggarwal et al., 2024).

GEO overlaps with SEO, because Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT search still read indexed pages. But it adds two layers that SEO retainers often skip: measuring answers instead of rankings, and building mentions of your brand on other sites. That second layer matters. In an Ahrefs analysis of 75,000 brands (2025), branded web mentions correlated with AI Overview visibility at 0.664, against 0.218 for backlinks.

Why does the wrong GEO agency cost you pipeline?

The wrong GEO agency is one that bills for reports and volume while your brand stays missing from AI answers buyers use for shortlists.

Volume is the risk most buyers underestimate. Google's spam policies list using generative AI tools to generate many pages without adding value as scaled content abuse, and warn that sites breaking the policies may rank lower or disappear from results (Google Search Central). An agency that pushes dozens of AI articles a month can damage the domain your AI visibility depends on.

The other failure is a report without fixes. A prompt audit tells you where you are missing. Nothing changes until someone edits pages, opens crawler access and earns mentions.

Which 10 questions should you ask a GEO agency?

Vetting a GEO agency means asking ten questions about prompts, measurement, technical access, content quality, authority, attribution and pricing before you sign.

  1. Which prompts will you track, and how did you pick them? A good answer maps 50 to 200 prompts to your buying journey, from problem questions to "best [category] for [industry]" shortlists, in every language your buyers use. Generic prompts produce flattering, useless scores.
  2. How do you measure AI visibility, and how often? Ask which assistants they cover (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, Google AI Overviews), which tool they use, and whether you get the raw answers. Forrester found business buyers are four times as likely as consumers to use Microsoft Copilot, so a B2B tracker without Copilot misses part of your market.
  3. Can AI crawlers read our site today? In Vercel's 2024 analysis, none of the major AI crawlers rendered JavaScript. The agency should check server-rendered HTML, robots.txt rules for OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot and PerplexityBot, and firewall settings in week one.
  4. What will you change on our site in the first 30 days? Expect named pages and named fixes: answer-first service pages, comparison tables, author pages, schema. A first month that only delivers a slide deck is a research project.
  5. How will you get our brand mentioned on other sites? Ask about digital PR, industry lists, review platforms, podcasts and communities, with examples of mentions they earned for other clients.
  6. Who writes the content, and who checks the facts? Google's guidance says it is critical to manually fact-check and review all AI-generated content (Google Search Central). You want named writers, a reviewer from your side, and a source for every number.
  7. How many pages will you publish per month, and why that number? The right number follows from your prompt gaps, not from a package. Be careful with any plan built on volume.
  8. How do you connect AI visibility to pipeline? AI answers often send no click. Good agencies combine share of voice with branded search trends, a "How did you hear about us?" field that lists AI assistants, and notes from sales calls.
  9. What exactly does the fee include? Separate the diagnostic, often a fixed-price sprint, from the ongoing retainer, and check who implements: the agency, your developers, or both.
  10. What do we keep if we stop? You should own the prompt set, the raw answer data, the dashboards and every page. If tracking lives only in the agency's tool account, your baseline leaves with them.

How is a GEO agency different from an SEO agency or an in-house team?

A GEO agency is different from an SEO agency in what it measures: citations inside AI answers, not rankings and clicks in search results.

What red flags should end the conversation?

A GEO red flag is any promise you cannot verify, such as guaranteed ChatGPT rankings, secret prompt hacks, or content packages priced per hundred articles.

  • Guaranteed positions in ChatGPT or Gemini. Answers vary by user, location and model version.
  • A visibility score without access to the raw answers behind it.
  • Content packages priced by volume.
  • No technical check of rendering and bot access.
  • A contract that keeps your tracking data with the agency.

Frequently asked questions

How long does GEO take to show results?

Assistants that search the live web, such as Perplexity and ChatGPT search, can pick up fixed pages within weeks. What models know from training changes more slowly, because it depends on new model versions.

Can my SEO agency also do GEO?

Often partly. Technical SEO, structured content and indexation carry over. Check whether they track AI answers per prompt and build brand mentions, the two parts SEO retainers usually leave out.

Which AI assistants matter most for B2B?

Start where your buyers work. Forrester found business buyers are twice as likely as consumers to use ChatGPT and four times as likely to use Microsoft Copilot, and 61% use private AI tools provided by their organization.

What does a GEO agency cost?

Prices vary with scope, markets and how much implementation is included. Ask for a fixed-scope diagnostic first, then a retainer with named monthly deliverables. Our own pricing is on the services page.

Is GEO the same as AEO?

They overlap. AEO, answer engine optimization, focuses on being the direct answer to a question. GEO covers the wider task of being mentioned and cited by generative AI, including your reputation on other sites. We compare all three in GEO vs SEO vs AEO.

Do we need GEO if our sales cycle is long and relationship-driven?

Yes. Shortlists form before the first sales call, and Forrester reports that 94% of business buyers use AI in their buying process.
