What is an AI visibility sprint?

An AI visibility sprint is a fixed 30-day program that measures where AI assistants mention your brand, fixes the blockers and sets up monthly tracking.

It has a fixed scope and a fixed end date. You get a baseline you can rerun, a list of fixes with owners, the first fixes shipped, and a tracking setup you keep.

Why start with a sprint instead of a retainer?

Starting with a sprint means you pay for a measured baseline and first fixes before committing budget to months of work you cannot yet judge.

GEO retainers are easy to sell and hard to evaluate. A sprint gives you hard numbers from your own prompts, so you can decide on a retainer, an in-house plan or both with data instead of promises.

What happens in each week of the sprint?

A sprint week is a block with one job: measure, fix access and structure, rewrite key pages, then build authority and reporting.

Which prompts should a B2B company track?

A B2B prompt set is a list of real buyer questions across three stages: problem questions, solution comparisons and vendor shortlists.

  • Problem: "Why is our pipeline from organic search shrinking?"
  • Solution: "What is the difference between GEO and SEO for B2B companies?"
  • Shortlist: "Best security awareness training providers for Dutch financial services"

Write the prompts in every language your buyers use, and include Microsoft Copilot. Forrester found business buyers are four times as likely as consumers to use Copilot. Then look at which sites the assistants cite. In an Ahrefs study of 75,000 brands, branded web mentions correlated with AI Overview visibility at 0.664, against 0.218 for backlinks. That is why week four goes into mentions, not only links. If you want to try the baseline yourself first, see how to audit AI visibility without paying for Profound.

How do you measure the result after 30 days?

Measuring a sprint means comparing the week-one baseline with a week-four rerun of the same prompts, plus crawler access, branded search and sales feedback.

Be realistic about the timeline. Assistants that search the live web can pick up fixed pages within weeks. What models remember from training moves more slowly. So judge the sprint on fixes shipped and early movement in retrieval-based answers, then track monthly.

Add one question to your forms and sales calls: "How did you hear about us?", with ChatGPT, Perplexity and Copilot as options. It is the simplest attribution you will get for answers that send no click. For more methods, read how to measure brand lift from LLM mentions.
