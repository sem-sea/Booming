Key takeaways

Across 75,000 brands, branded web mentions correlated with AI Overview visibility at 0.664 and backlinks at 0.218 (Ahrefs, 2025). Links still help pages rank. AI visibility follows how often and where the web talks about you.

For twenty years, link building was the core of off-site SEO. AI search shifts the weight. In Ahrefs' May 2025 analysis, the three strongest factors for AI Overview visibility were all brand signals: branded web mentions (0.664), branded anchors (0.527) and branded search volume (0.392) (Ahrefs).

A December 2025 follow-up across ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and AI Overviews found YouTube mentions the strongest single factor, at about 0.737, with Domain Rating far weaker (Ahrefs). These are correlations, not proof of cause, but the pattern is consistent.

Brand perception in AI is now a board topic. Gartner found in November 2026 that 42% of CMOs actively monitor and correct misinformation about their brand in AI-generated output.

What is a brand mention in AI search?

A brand mention is any place on the web that names your company, with or without a link: articles, reviews, forums, podcasts and video.

The link is optional. A trade article that names you as an example, a Reddit thread that recommends you, a podcast transcript or a review on G2 all count, linked or not.

Why do mentions matter more than links for AI?

The mention effect means AI models link brands to topics through how often and where they are named, which backlink counts miss.

How should B2B teams split budget between PR and links?

A sensible split is one that funds earned mentions first, through research, PR and reviews, and keeps link building for pages that must rank.

Most B2B teams can shift part of a link budget to mention-building; press coverage and quoted research often bring links as well. Six channels to use:

  1. Original research that journalists and analysts quote: a benchmark, a survey, an index.
  2. Expert commentary in trade media, such as columns and quotes in roundups.
  3. Podcasts and YouTube, given the strong YouTube correlation in the Ahrefs data.
  4. Reviews on the platforms your buyers use.
  5. Communities such as LinkedIn and relevant subreddits, with real answers, not promotion.
  6. Partner pages: integration directories, case studies on partner sites, joint webinars.

How do you measure mentions?

Measuring mentions means tracking named references across the web and in AI answers each month, then comparing your share against three main competitors.

Use a media monitoring tool for web mentions and an AI visibility tracker for answers. Report both as a share against the same competitors, so the trend means something. Our post on measuring brand lift from LLM mentions goes deeper.

Frequently asked questions

Do unlinked mentions help SEO?

Google has not confirmed a direct ranking effect. For AI visibility, the Ahrefs data shows branded mentions, linked or not, correlate far more strongly than backlinks.

Should we stop link building?

No. Pages still need links to rank in Google, and Google results feed AI Overviews. Shift the balance, not the whole budget.

Do negative mentions hurt?

They can shape how assistants describe you. Gartner found 42% of CMOs now monitor and correct misinformation in AI output. Track sentiment, not only volume.

Does YouTube matter for B2B?

In the December 2025 Ahrefs study, YouTube mentions had the strongest correlation with AI visibility, at about 0.737. Talks, demos and podcast clips on YouTube count.

How long before mentions show up in AI answers?

Assistants that search the web can pick up new mentions within weeks. Model knowledge from training updates only with new model versions.
