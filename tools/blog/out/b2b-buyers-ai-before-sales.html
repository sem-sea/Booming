Key takeaways

45% of B2B buyers used generative AI in a recent purchase, and 69% prefer to validate AI-generated insights with a sales rep (Gartner, 2026). Buyers research with AI, check with peers and reviews, and come to sales with a shortlist.

Gartner surveyed 645 B2B buyers in August and September 2025. They used an average of seven information sources in a recent purchase; 45% used generative AI, mainly to research vendors and products (Gartner).

They prefer to do it alone. In a related Gartner survey, 67% of B2B buyers preferred a buying experience without a sales rep (Gartner).

But they check what AI tells them. TrustRadius found that 63% of technology buyers used AI during their purchase journey, and 94% of those fact-check its answers at least some of the time (TrustRadius, 2026).

What does the AI-assisted B2B buying journey look like?

The AI-assisted buying journey is research by assistant, validation through peers and reviews, and a sales conversation that starts with a shortlist already made.

Why do buyers still want sales reps?

The main reason buyers want sales reps is validation: about half expect misleading information from generative AI, so reps confirm facts before commitment.

Gartner found 51% of buyers expect more misleading information from generative AI, and 49% expect it from sales reps. Reps matter most when buyers research a business problem, choose a preferred supplier, secure internal support and finalize the purchase.

What should B2B marketing do differently?

Marketing for AI-assisted buyers means making your facts easy for assistants to find, easy for buyers to verify, and easy for reps to confirm.

  1. Check how AI describes you for the questions buyers ask at each stage.
  2. Publish verifiable facts: pricing approach, integrations, certifications, customers.
  3. Invest in reviews; TrustRadius found 74% of buyers use them in purchase decisions.
  4. Brief sales on what AI says about you and your competitors.
  5. Ask every new lead which AI tools they used, and log it in the CRM.

Frequently asked questions

How many B2B buyers use AI?

45% in Gartner's survey of 645 buyers and 63% in TrustRadius' survey of 1,862 technology buyers.

Do buyers trust AI answers?

Partly. 94% of AI users in the TrustRadius study fact-check answers at least some of the time.

Do buyers still want to talk to sales?

67% prefer a rep-free experience overall, but 69% want reps to validate AI-generated insights.

Which sources do buyers trust most?

TrustRadius found demos, free trials, prior experience and user reviews among the most influential resources.

What should sales teams change?

Expect buyers who arrive with a shortlist and AI-sourced claims. Prepare to confirm or correct them with evidence.
