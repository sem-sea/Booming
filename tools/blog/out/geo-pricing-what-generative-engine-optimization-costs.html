Key takeaways

The median GEO or AEO retainer was $6,850 a month in First Page Sage's October 2026 study of 1,146 price quotes. The price follows scope: markets, languages, prompts tracked, and how much implementation and outreach the agency does itself.

GEO budgets are moving fast. First Page Sage collected 1,146 quotes from 214 agencies between July and October 2026. The median GEO retainer of $6,850 a month sits well above the median SEO retainer of $4,740, and up from $4,120 for GEO in early 2024 (First Page Sage). First Page Sage is itself a GEO agency, so treat these figures as a market reference.

Prices in Europe can start lower. Dutch agency Searchlab lists AI visibility work from €1,500 a month, cancellable monthly (Searchlab). Tools are the smallest line: Peec AI charges $95 a month for 50 tracked prompts and $495 for 350.

The reason buyers pay at all: Forrester reports that 94% of business buyers use AI in their buying process. This guide shows what you pay for and how to compare proposals.

What is GEO pricing based on?

GEO pricing is the fee for tracking, fixing and growing how AI assistants mention your brand, set mainly by scope, markets and implementation work.

Six things move the price. Most proposals bundle them, so ask for each line separately.

  1. Prompts and assistants tracked. Running 50 prompts in three assistants costs far less than running 300 prompts in five.
  2. Markets and languages. Every language needs its own prompt set, its own pages and its own sources.
  3. Implementation. An agency that edits your pages and schema itself costs more than one that sends recommendations to your developers.
  4. Content production. New answer pages and comparison pages take writers and subject experts.
  5. Mentions on other sites. Digital PR, list placements and review programs are often the most expensive line, and often the most effective.
  6. Reporting and tools. Tracker licenses and monthly reporting time are usually included, but check whether you keep access to the data.

Which pricing models do GEO agencies use?

A GEO pricing model is the way an agency bills: a monthly retainer, a fixed-scope sprint, a one-off audit, hourly consulting or a performance fee.

How much should a B2B company budget?

A realistic GEO budget is the retainer for your company size plus tools, content and the internal hours needed to approve and publish changes.

By client size, First Page Sage found medians of $2,950 a month for small businesses, $7,400 for mid-market, $14,600 for upper mid-market and $31,500 for enterprise. Financial services had the highest industry median at $9,850 a month.

Add three costs that rarely appear in a proposal. Tool licenses, if you want your own access. Writer or expert time for new pages. And internal time: someone in your team has to approve changes, or nothing ships. Budget a few hours a week for that.

What should a GEO proposal include?

A complete GEO proposal is one that names the prompts, assistants, monthly deliverables, owners of implementation and the reporting you keep after the contract ends.

  • The prompt set, or how it will be built in week one, in every language your buyers use.
  • The assistants covered: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot and Google AI Overviews.
  • Named monthly deliverables, such as pages rewritten, mentions earned and reports.
  • Who implements: the agency, your developers or both, with hours.
  • Data ownership: prompt set, raw answers and dashboards stay with you.

Be careful with proposals priced by the number of AI articles. Google's spam policies call using generative AI to create many pages without added value scaled content abuse (Google Search Central).

Is a sprint cheaper than a retainer?

A GEO sprint is a fixed-scope project, usually 30 days, that buys a baseline and first fixes before you commit to a monthly retainer.

Per month, a sprint is often more expensive, because the first weeks carry the setup work. In total, it is cheaper than a retainer you stop after three months because you could not judge it. Our 30-day AI visibility sprint plan shows what a sprint should deliver week by week.

Frequently asked questions

Why do GEO retainers cost more than SEO retainers?

GEO adds work that SEO retainers rarely include: running and analyzing prompts across several assistants, and earning mentions on other sites. First Page Sage found a median of $6,850 a month for GEO against $4,740 for SEO.

Can we do GEO with tools only?

Tools show where you appear. They do not rewrite pages, open crawler access or earn mentions. Many in-house teams start with a tracker such as Peec AI or Otterly.ai and buy help for the parts they cannot staff.

Are GEO prices lower in Europe?

Some are. Searchlab in the Netherlands starts at €1,500 a month for AI visibility as a standalone line. Compare scope, not only price.
