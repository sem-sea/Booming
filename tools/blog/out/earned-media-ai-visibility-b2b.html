Key takeaways

84% of AI citations come from earned media (Muck Rack, May 2026), and news mentions correlate with AI visibility at 0.67 (Ahrefs, 2026). Earn mentions in the trade media, communities and lists that assistants already cite for your category.

Owned content is a small part of what AI assistants cite. Muck Rack's analysis of more than 25 million links found earned media made up 84% of citations, with owned and paid content at about 16% (Muck Rack).

Mentions track visibility. In an Ahrefs study of 10,000 brands and 260 million AI prompts, mentions in Reddit ranking threads correlated with AI mentions at 0.75, webpage mentions at 0.70 and news article mentions at 0.67 (Ahrefs).

And the journalists who matter may not be on your list. Muck Rack found an average overlap of just 2% between journalists brands pitched and journalists AI cited (GlobeNewswire release on Muck Rack data).

What is earned media in AI search?

Earned media in AI search means brand mentions you did not pay for or publish: articles, reviews, forum threads, lists and other companies' content.

The definition is broad on purpose. Muck Rack counts journalism, academic, government, encyclopedic, social and third-party corporate sources.

Where should B2B brands earn mentions?

The best place for a mention is a source assistants already cite for your buyer prompts, found by running those prompts and logging citations.

How do you pitch for mentions that AI cites?

Pitching for AI-cited mentions means targeting the outlets and authors assistants already cite, and offering them data or expertise they cannot get elsewhere.

  1. Build your target list from AI answers, not only from a media database.
  2. Lead with data: a benchmark, a survey, anonymized results.
  3. Offer an expert with a clear point of view and availability.
  4. Make facts easy to check with a source page on your site.
  5. Follow up on lists and roundups before they refresh.
  6. Track AI mentions monthly to see which placements moved the needle.

Frequently asked questions

Does small coverage count?

Ahrefs found traffic-weighted mentions correlated about as strongly as raw mentions, so a mention on a smaller relevant site still counts.

Is earned media more important than our own site?

Both matter. Your site supplies the facts; earned media tells assistants others trust you.

How many mentions do we need?

Ahrefs found returns diminish: ten times more webpage mentions went with about 3.7 times more AI mentions.

Should B2B brands be on Reddit?

Where buyers discuss your category, yes, as genuine participants. Promotion gets removed and hurts trust.

Can an agency do this for us?

Yes, as long as they pitch real stories and data. Mass outreach rarely earns coverage.
