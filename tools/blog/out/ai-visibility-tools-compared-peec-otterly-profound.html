What is an AI visibility tool?

An AI visibility tool is software that runs your buyer prompts in assistants like ChatGPT and Perplexity on a schedule and records who gets named.

Most tools report three things: whether your brand appears, which competitors appear instead, and which sources the assistant cited. The source list is often the most useful part, because it tells you where to earn mentions.

How do the main tools compare?

Comparing AI visibility tools means checking prompt capacity, engines covered, data export and price per prompt, since dashboards look similar across vendors.

Which tool fits a B2B team?

The right tool for a B2B team is one that covers your buyers' assistants and languages at a prompt volume you can review monthly.

  1. Count your prompts first. 50 to 150 buyer prompts per market is a common starting set.
  2. List the engines your buyers use. For B2B, include Microsoft Copilot next to ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini.
  3. Check data export. You should be able to download raw answers and sources.
  4. Check competitor tracking. Share of voice needs at least three competitors on the same prompts.
  5. Add Bing Webmaster Tools regardless; it costs nothing and covers Copilot.
  6. Run a one-month trial on your real prompts before signing an annual plan.

Frequently asked questions

Do we need a paid tool at all?

For a first look, running 20 buyer prompts by hand works. For monthly reporting across several assistants and competitors, a tool saves days of work.

Which tool does Booming Venture use?

We run prompt sets in Otterly.ai or Peec AI, depending on the client's engines and volume, plus Bing Webmaster Tools and Google Search Console.

Are the results reliable?

AI answers vary between runs. Good tools run prompts repeatedly and report trends; treat any single result as a sample.

How much should a mid-size B2B company spend on tools?

Entry plans start around $95 to $99 a month. Nutshell's 2026 overview puts AI visibility tracking at $50 to over $1,000 a month depending on scale.

Can these tools improve visibility?

No. They measure. Improvement comes from fixing pages, crawler access and mentions on other sites.
