Key takeaways

Buyers want prices and AI assistants answer price questions anyway: transparent pricing has been buyers' top wish-list item since 2023 (TrustRadius, 2026). If you do not publish a price or a pricing model, assistants will describe you from someone else's numbers.

Pricing transparency is the most requested change in B2B buying. In TrustRadius' January 2025 survey of 2,058 technology buyers, 45% named it the number one change they want from vendors (eMarketer on TrustRadius data).

It has stayed at the top of the list since 2023, according to TrustRadius' 2026 report, which surveyed 1,862 buyers (TrustRadius).

Buyers want to do the work themselves: Gartner found 70% prefer a fully digital, self-service buying experience.

What does AI search change about pricing?

The pricing shift is that buyers now ask assistants "how much does X cost?" and get an answer, whether or not you published one.

Without a source on your site, the assistant relies on reviews, old articles or competitor pages. That is how outdated or wrong prices end up in shortlists.

Which pricing information should you publish?

Publishable pricing means at least your pricing model and a starting point, even if final quotes depend on scope, users or volume.

How do you publish pricing without losing deals?

Publishing pricing safely means explaining what drives the price, showing a starting point, and making it easy to ask for a quote.

  1. State your pricing model in one sentence at the top of the page.
  2. Show a starting price or typical range per plan or service.
  3. List what drives the price: users, volume, markets, implementation.
  4. Explain what is included and what costs extra.
  5. Date the page and update it with every change.
  6. Add a clear next step: a calculator, a quote request or a call.

Frequently asked questions

Will competitors use our prices against us?

They can find them anyway through buyers. Being clear about what is included lets you compete on value, not on a hidden number.

What if every deal is custom?

Publish the pricing model and typical ranges. Buyers mainly want to know if you fit their budget.

Does a pricing page help AI visibility?

It gives assistants a first-party source for price questions, instead of third-party guesses.

How often should pricing pages be updated?

With every change, plus a quarterly review. Outdated prices cause more harm than none.

Do buyers really shortlist on price?

TrustRadius found 83% of buyers shortlist three or fewer products. With a list that short, unclear pricing can cost you a place on it.
