Key takeaways

ChatGPT reached 900 million weekly active users in February 2026 (TechCrunch) and sends 81% of AI referral visits to B2B websites (Leadfeeder, 2026). To get cited, your pages must be open to OAI-SearchBot, readable without JavaScript and direct in how they answer.

For most B2B companies, ChatGPT is the only AI assistant that sends real traffic. In Leadfeeder's October 2026 study, ChatGPT accounted for 81% of AI referral visits to B2B sites, ahead of Gemini at 8% and Claude at 5% (Leadfeeder).

Those visits convert. Orbit Media analyzed 97 B2B and lead generation sites and found ChatGPT visitors converted at about 2.1%, against 0.5% for Google search, although Google still sent around 100 times more visits (Orbit Media).

Volume is rising too. Demandbase measured a 303% increase in monthly ChatGPT referrals to B2B sites between June 2025 and June 2026 (PPC Land on Demandbase data).

What is ChatGPT search?

ChatGPT search is the feature that lets ChatGPT look up current web pages and cite them as linked sources inside its answers.

It relies on OpenAI's own crawler. OpenAI says sites that opt out of OAI-SearchBot will not appear in ChatGPT search answers, though they can still show up as navigational links (OpenAI).

How does ChatGPT choose which pages to cite?

ChatGPT's source choice means picking pages it can crawl, that match the question closely and that look current; OpenAI does not publish a ranking formula.

Freshness is one factor you can measure. In an Ahrefs study of 17 million citations, ChatGPT showed the strongest preference for newer content of all assistants tested: its citations were on average 458 days newer than Google's organic results (Ahrefs, 2025).

Citations are routine, not rare. Muck Rack found ChatGPT includes citations in 96% of responses, about five per answer, with Wikipedia as its most cited domain (Muck Rack, May 2026).

What should B2B teams fix first?

The first fix for ChatGPT visibility is access: allow OAI-SearchBot, serve full HTML, and make sure your key pages answer buyer questions directly.

  1. Allow OAI-SearchBot in robots.txt and in your firewall or CDN settings.
  2. Decide on GPTBot separately. It collects training data. OpenAI says each crawler setting is independent of the others.
  3. Serve full HTML. In Vercel's analysis, none of the major AI crawlers rendered JavaScript.
  4. Answer buyer questions on the page: a question-shaped heading, then the answer in two sentences.
  5. Refresh your key pages with new facts and a visible update date.
  6. Earn mentions on the sites ChatGPT already cites for your category: review platforms, trade media and lists.

How do you measure ChatGPT visibility?

Measuring ChatGPT visibility means tracking two numbers: how often you are named for buyer prompts, and how many visits and leads ChatGPT sends.

Frequently asked questions

Does blocking GPTBot remove us from ChatGPT search?

No. OpenAI says the crawler settings are independent, so you can allow OAI-SearchBot for search while disallowing GPTBot for training.

Is ChatGPT traffic worth the effort for B2B?

It is small but valuable. Orbit Media found it converts about four times better than Google search, while Leadfeeder found a slightly lower share of visitors who match the ideal customer profile than organic search.

How fast do changes show up in ChatGPT?

Pages that OAI-SearchBot can crawl can be picked up within weeks. Knowledge from model training changes only with new model versions.

Do we need a separate ChatGPT strategy?

Mostly not. Crawlable pages, clear answers and mentions on trusted sites help in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI features alike.

Which pages does ChatGPT cite most?

In Muck Rack's data, Wikipedia is its most cited domain. For your category, run your buyer prompts and record the sources yourself.
