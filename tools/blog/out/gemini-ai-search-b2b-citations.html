Key takeaways

Gemini cites sources in 82% of answers, about eight per answer, with Reddit as its most cited domain (Muck Rack, May 2026). It also had the most accuracy issues in an EBU and BBC study. Check what Gemini says about you and strengthen the sources it reads.

Gemini draws on Google's index, but its citations look different from Google's results. In Muck Rack's analysis of more than 25 million links, Reddit made up about 2.4% of Gemini's citations, the largest single domain, with Forbes as its top journalism outlet (Muck Rack).

Accuracy is a concern. In the EBU and BBC study of more than 3,000 answers in 14 languages, Gemini had significant issues in 76% of its responses, more than double the other assistants tested (EBU).

It is the second AI referral source for B2B sites, at 8% of AI referral visits, well behind ChatGPT at 81% (Leadfeeder, 2026).

What is Gemini's search grounding?

Gemini's grounding is the process of supplying content from Google's search index to the model when it answers, so responses can cite current web pages.

Google says the Google-Extended robots.txt token controls whether your content is used for training future Gemini models and for grounding in Gemini Apps, and that it does not affect inclusion in Google Search (Google).

How does Gemini choose its sources?

Gemini's source choice means drawing on Google's index while often citing community threads and pages fresher than Google's organic results.

Freshness plays a role. In an Ahrefs study of 17 million citations, content Gemini cited was on average 298 days newer than Google's organic results.

What should B2B brands do about Gemini?

Working on Gemini visibility means keeping Google-Extended open if you want grounding, earning community mentions, and checking Gemini's answers about you for errors.

  1. Decide on Google-Extended deliberately. Blocking it removes your content from Gemini grounding.
  2. Take part in relevant Reddit discussions as a genuine expert.
  3. Keep key facts fresh and clear on your own pages.
  4. Fact-check Gemini monthly on pricing, features and certifications.
  5. Report wrong answers with Gemini's feedback option and fix the source pages.

Frequently asked questions

Does blocking Google-Extended hurt our Google rankings?

Google says it does not affect inclusion in Google Search and is not used as a ranking factor.

Why does Gemini cite Reddit so often?

Reddit was its most cited domain in Muck Rack's data. Community threads answer comparison questions in buyers' own words.

Is Gemini reliable enough to matter?

Its error rate was high in the EBU study, which is exactly why brands should check what it says about them.

How much traffic does Gemini send?

About 8% of AI referral visits to B2B sites in Leadfeeder's 2026 data.

Is Gemini the same as Google AI Overviews?

Both draw on Google's models and index, but they are separate products with different interfaces.
