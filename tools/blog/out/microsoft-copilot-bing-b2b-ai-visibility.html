Key takeaways

Forrester found business buyers four times as likely as consumers to use Microsoft Copilot (2026). Copilot sends its web queries to Bing, and since February 2026 Bing Webmaster Tools shows how often Copilot cites your pages.

Most GEO advice starts with ChatGPT. For B2B, that leaves out the assistant built into the software your buyers use all day. Forrester also found that 61% of business buyers use private AI tools provided by their organization (Forrester).

Copilot's web answers run on Bing. Microsoft says Copilot generates a short query from your prompt and sends it to Bing, and a Sources button shows the query and the sources used (Microsoft Support).

And Microsoft now shares the data. Since 10 February 2026, the AI Performance report in Bing Webmaster Tools shows citations in Copilot, AI summaries in Bing and select partners (Bing Webmaster Blog).

What is Copilot visibility?

Copilot visibility is how often Microsoft Copilot cites or names your company in answers, which depends largely on how Bing indexes and ranks your pages.

Bing has its own index. A page that ranks in Google is not automatically indexed or ranked in Bing, so Copilot can miss you even when Google does not.

Why does Copilot matter more in B2B than in consumer markets?

Copilot's B2B weight means it sits inside Microsoft 365, where many business buyers already work, so research happens next to email, documents and Teams.

A buyer preparing a vendor comparison in Word or a summary for a buying committee can ask Copilot without opening a browser. In organizations that allow web search in Copilot Chat, those answers draw on Bing.

How do you see your Copilot citations?

Seeing Copilot citations means using the AI Performance report in Bing Webmaster Tools, which counts citations, cited pages and grounding queries for your site.

How do you improve visibility in Copilot?

Improving Copilot visibility means getting indexed and ranked well in Bing: verified site, fresh sitemap, IndexNow, crawlable HTML and pages that answer business questions.

  1. Verify your site in Bing Webmaster Tools. You can import it from Google Search Console in a few clicks.
  2. Submit your XML sitemap and check that the important pages are indexed.
  3. Turn on IndexNow, so Bing hears about new and updated pages right away.
  4. Check that Bingbot is not blocked by robots.txt, a firewall or rate limiting.
  5. Read your grounding queries and map each one to the page that should answer it.
  6. Strengthen the pages behind commercial intents: comparisons, pricing, integrations, case studies.
  7. Track monthly with Compare, and add Copilot to your prompt tracking tool.

Frequently asked questions

Is Copilot the same as Bing Chat?

Microsoft renamed Bing Chat to Copilot in late 2023. Today Copilot appears in Windows, Edge, Bing and Microsoft 365.

Does Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat use the web?

Yes, when web search is turned on. Administrators can turn it off for their organization, and users can switch it off in their settings.

Does the AI Performance report show rankings?

No. Microsoft says it shows how often content is cited, not ranking, authority or placement within an answer.

Does a good Google ranking carry over to Bing?

Not automatically. Bing crawls and ranks with its own systems, so check indexing in Bing Webmaster Tools separately.

Can we track private Copilot use by buyers?

Not directly. Buyers who read a Copilot answer and never click leave no trace on your site. A "How did you hear about us?" field that lists AI assistants is the practical proxy.
