Key takeaways

Data gets cited: adding statistics improved visibility in generative engine answers by about 31% in the GEO study (KDD 2024), and research reports had the strongest direct impact on security vendor selection, at 46% (Ponemon and NOLA, 2026). Original research works as a lead magnet and a citation magnet.

The researchers behind the GEO study tested content changes on 10,000 queries. Adding quotations lifted visibility by about 41%, adding statistics by about 31% and citing sources by about 28%; keyword stuffing made visibility worse (Aggarwal et al.).

Buyers value research too. In a June 2026 study of 320 enterprise security decision-makers, research and survey reports had the strongest direct impact on selection at 46% (Ponemon and NOLA).

And research earns coverage. Journalism made up about 27% of AI citations in Muck Rack's May 2026 data (Muck Rack), and journalists need data.

What is original research in B2B marketing?

Original research is data your company collects and publishes, such as a survey, a benchmark from anonymized client data, or an index tracked over time.

It does not need to be large. A survey of 200 buyers in one sector, done well, is more useful than a generic report everyone has seen.

Why does original research earn AI citations?

Research earns citations because it is a primary source: assistants and journalists quoting a specific number need someone to attribute it to.

Every statistic in an AI answer comes from somewhere. If your benchmark is the only source for a number in your niche, you become the citation.

How do you plan research that generates leads?

Planning lead-generating research means choosing a question buyers care about, collecting credible data, and publishing findings openly while gating the full report.

  1. Pick one question your buyers argue about, such as costs, benchmarks or adoption rates.
  2. Choose a credible method: a sample size, a period and a clear definition.
  3. Publish key findings openly on an HTML page with charts and method.
  4. Gate the full report for leads, if you want, but keep the headline numbers open.
  5. Pitch the findings to trade media and analysts.
  6. Repeat yearly, so the trend becomes the reason to cite you.

Frequently asked questions

How big does a survey need to be?

Big enough to be credible for your niche. Report the sample size and method so readers can judge.

Should we gate the report?

Gate the full PDF if leads matter, but keep key numbers on an open page. Assistants cannot cite what they cannot read.

Can we use our own client data?

Yes, anonymized and aggregated, with client consent where contracts require it.

How long does a research project take?

A focused survey takes four to eight weeks from questions to published findings.

What if the results are boring?

Then the angle is wrong. Pick a question where the answer is uncertain or disputed.
