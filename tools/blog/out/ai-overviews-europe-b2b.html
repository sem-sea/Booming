Key takeaways

Google AI Overviews launched in Dutch in May 2025 (Google Netherlands) after a March 2025 rollout to nine more European countries (Google). Where they appear, the top result loses clicks: Ahrefs measured a 58% lower click-through rate.

Europe got AI Overviews later than the US. In March 2025, Google added Austria, Belgium, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Poland, Portugal, Spain and Switzerland. Belgium launched with English only (Google).

Dutch followed on 20 May 2025, announced at Google I/O (Google Netherlands). For B2B companies in the Benelux, that means many of the search markets they sell in now show AI summaries.

The effect on clicks is measurable. Pew Research Center found that when Google showed an AI summary, 8% of visits included a click on a result, against 15% without one (Pew Research Center).

What are AI Overviews?

An AI Overview is an AI-generated summary at the top of Google search results that answers the query and links to a few source pages.

They draw on Google's index. Pages that rank and answer clearly have a better chance of being linked, but many users read the summary and stop there.

Where do AI Overviews appear in Europe?

AI Overview coverage in Europe is broad: most large EU markets, including the Netherlands in Dutch, Germany in German and Belgium in English.

How should B2B companies respond?

Responding to AI Overviews means measuring which of your queries trigger them, structuring pages to be the cited source, and building demand beyond clicks.

  1. Check your top queries by market for AI Overviews, starting with commercial ones.
  2. Lead pages with the answer in two sentences under a question-shaped heading.
  3. Strengthen pages that already rank, since Overviews draw on Google's index.
  4. Track branded search in Search Console per country as a demand signal.
  5. Earn mentions in local trade media in each market you sell in.

Frequently asked questions

Do AI Overviews appear for B2B queries?

Yes, especially for informational and comparison queries. Check your own queries per market.

Can we opt out of AI Overviews?

Google offers snippet controls such as nosnippet, but they also limit how your page appears in regular results. Use them with care.

Do AI Overviews show in every language in Belgium?

Belgium launched with English in March 2025. Check current availability in Dutch and French before planning.

How do we see AI Overview traffic?

Search Console reports it as part of web search; it is not separated. Track click-through trends per query.

Does this affect paid search?

Ads still appear around AI Overviews. Watch click-through on informational keywords and shift budget to commercial ones if needed.
