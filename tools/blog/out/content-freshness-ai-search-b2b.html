Key takeaways

AI assistants cite newer content than Google ranks: in an Ahrefs study of 17 million citations, cited pages were 25.7% fresher than organic results. Update your key commercial pages every quarter with real changes, not just a new date.

Freshness shows up across large citation studies. Ahrefs compared 17 million citations from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot and Google AI Overviews with Google's organic results: on average, AI-cited content was 25.7% fresher (Ahrefs, 2025).

Lists show the same pattern. Of 1,100 blog lists ChatGPT cited, 79.1% had been updated in 2025, and 26% in the two months before the study (Ahrefs).

Journalism too: 57% of journalism citations with a known date were published in the last 12 months, and citations peak in the first month after publication (Muck Rack, May 2026).

What is content freshness in AI search?

Content freshness is how recently a page was published or meaningfully updated, which AI assistants appear to weigh when choosing sources to cite.

Not every assistant behaves the same. Ahrefs found ChatGPT had the strongest preference for newer content, while Google AI Overviews was the only system that cited slightly older content than Google's organic results.

Which B2B pages should you update first?

Your first update priority is the pages buyers use to decide: pricing, comparisons, product pages, integration pages and your most visited guides.

What counts as a real update?

A real update means the content changed in substance: new data, corrected facts, new sections or removed outdated material, not only a changed date.

  1. Replace outdated statistics with the latest data and link the source.
  2. Add what changed: new features, prices, regulations or market data.
  3. Remove what is no longer true, including discontinued products.
  4. Show the update date visibly on the page.
  5. Notify search engines through your sitemap and IndexNow, which Bing supports.

Frequently asked questions

Does changing the date alone help?

It is not a real update and it misleads readers. Change the substance, then update the date.

Do all AI assistants prefer fresh content?

Most do in the Ahrefs data. ChatGPT showed the strongest preference; Google AI Overviews did not prefer newer content.

Should we update old blog posts or write new ones?

Update posts that still serve buyers. Merge or remove thin ones. New posts make sense for new questions.

How many pages can a small team keep fresh?

Focus on 10 to 20 commercial pages. A quarterly review of those beats a yearly sweep of everything.

Does freshness matter for evergreen topics?

Less, but cited content in the Ahrefs study was still fresher on average. Re-check facts at least yearly.
